# Automation Scan — Sample Report

> **Illustration.** This is a fictional company built with the same structure, scoring

---

## 1. Executive summary

**Achterhuis & Zonen — 4-person plumbing and heating service business, Rotterdam.**

Three leaks dominate: manual quoting, one mixed inbox, and no lead capture on the site.
Together they cost roughly **9 hours per week** of owner and office time — about
**€37K per year** at a conservative €80/h. The top fix (quoted, gated inbox + quote flow)
pays back in weeks. Recommendation: **build the Quote & Order Engine first.**

---

## 2. How the scan ran

- 90-minute session, owner + office manager, pre-work review of the site and quote history.
- Reviewed: inbox, quoting path, site lead flow, reporting.
- All numbers below are what was *measured* during the session, not estimates from a template.

---

## 3. Scored maturity

Five dimensions, 1–5 (5 = controlled system, 1 = manual/scattered).

| Dimension | Score | What we saw |
|---|---|---|
| Tools & integrations | 2 | Email + Excel + a paper quote book |
| Processes | 2 | Quote steps exist but vary every time |
| Data | 2 | Same customer data typed in 3 places |
| Automation | 1 | One mail merge rule, nothing else |
| People & decision | 3 | Owner is the bottleneck on every quote |

The imbalance is the story: **people carry the load, systems carry nothing.**

## 5. Quantified payback

Math shown per item, not asserted. Owner time valued at €80/h (conservative for the trade).

| Leak | Hours/week | × 47 wk | × €80 | Yearly |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Manual quotes | 4 h | 188 h | €80 | €15,040 |
| Inbox triage by hand | 3 h | 141 h | €80 | €11,280 |
| Site leads missed | ~2/mo | — | — | ~€5–10K lost revenue |
| Reporting by hand | 1.5 h | 70 h | €80 | €5,640 |

**Combined: ~9 h/week ≈ €37K/year.** The scan fee is credited toward the build that removes the first leak.

## 6. Roadmap (30/60/90)

| Phase | What | Owner involvement |
|---|---|---|
| 0–30 days | Quote & Order Engine — one guided quote path, price before pay | Approve the price list once |
| 30–60 days | Inbox triage — lanes + drafts, you approve each send | Approve sends |
| 60–90 days | Site lead capture + lead handoff into the quote flow | Review captured leads weekly |

Nothing ships without your click. Every action is logged, every release rollback-able.

---

## 7. Go / no-go

**Go, scoped.** Quote & Order Engine first. Expected owner-hours saved after month one:
**~4 h/week** — the number is measured after go-live, not promised upfront.

**What we would NOT automate:** purchase-order matching (low volume, not worth the gate),
and we would say so even if you wanted it.
