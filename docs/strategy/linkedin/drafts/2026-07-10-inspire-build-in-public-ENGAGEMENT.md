# Engagement routine — 15 minutes (post-publish)

Run **after** you publish the INSPIRE carousel and post the first-comment CTA.

## Timer: 15 min

| Min | Action |
|-----|--------|
| 0–2 | Reply to **every** comment on your new post (even "nice" — ask one question back) |
| 2–10 | Find **3–5 posts** from NL ZZP / SMB owners (quotes, inbox, website leads) |
| 10–15 | Leave **value comments** (no pitch, no links) — see templates below |

## Comment reply templates (on your post)

**Generic praise:**
```text
Thanks — curious: do you quote mostly by email today or still a lot by phone?
```

**"What is INSPIRE?":**
```text
Short version: supervised intake → brief → two directions → inspiration mockups → wizard. Not print-ready AI art. Still tuning the visual bar — building in public.
```

**"DM me" / interest:**
```text
Happy to — what do you sell and where does the quote process get stuck today? (email ping-pong, no visual direction, pricing confusion?)
```

## ICP engagement comments (other people's posts)

Use when someone mentions manual quotes, inbox overload, or weak website conversion:

```text
Same pattern in my NL print business — the leak was not "more AI", it was mapping the quote path first. Sorting that changed what we automate next.
```

```text
That email ping-pong before scope is real. We moved structured intake + visual direction earlier in the flow — fewer redraw rounds later.
```

**Rules (LI-R13):** No Map CTA in other people's threads. No links.

## DM qualification (only after they engaged)

1. Context first: reference their comment or your post.
2. One question: *What do you sell and how do you quote today?*
3. Map CTA only if they describe a matching leak (manual quotes, no visual direction, scattered tools).

```text
If you want to map the leak before building — I start with a paid Automation Map (€290, credited toward build). No vague discovery call. Want the link?
```

## Done when

- [ ] First-comment CTA posted (within 2h of publish)
- [ ] All comments on your post answered
- [ ] 3+ meaningful comments on ICP posts
- [ ] DMs from business owners noted (not only builders)

Log result in personal notes or next handoff — no repo commit required unless you want CHANGELOG update.
